The Grand Heist at the Museum of Antiquities occurred on a rainy Tuesday night. 
Detective Pratap was the first to arrive at the scene, noticing the shattered glass near the main exhibit. 
Security footage showed a shadowed figure bypassing the alarms. 
Interrogations revealed that Kunal, the museum curator, had the only master key, but he claimed it was stolen from his desk. 
Meanwhile, Ravi, a notorious local smuggler, was seen purchasing a one-way ticket out of the city just hours before the break-in. 
However, it was Irfan, the quiet janitor, who secretly had access to the building's electrical grid and disabled the cameras from the basement.
